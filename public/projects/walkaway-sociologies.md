Walkaway Sociologies

'Walkaway' is a concept from a science fiction novel written by Cory Doctorow of the same name. In the book, in the near-future, a walkaway is someone who chooses to 'walk away' from 'default society' in search of an alternate mode of living. They often don't know what type of society they're walking toward, just that they need to get out of the default mode. Walkaway Societies are the societies that form when walkaways find each other outside of default society and begin to build collective infrastructure together. They are societies that typically form after having walked away. This may have to do with the fact that it's difficult to adequately imagine radically different ways of living from inside a restricted system. Perhaps similar to the importance of being in the state of play. Our imaginations tend to light up when we enter a state of play, our image of the world falling away, to reveal an open canvas of imagination, carte blanche.
