Ethereum Extitutional has its sights on an informal space of cultural (peer) production in Ethereum, a space of extitutional production.

The Cyborg Anthro Wiki defines Extitutional theory as follows:
"Extitutional theory examines how social phenomena emerge and are sustained through local interactions and mutual recognition, rather than through formal roles and rules. An extitution forms through the natural alignment of participants' behaviors and relationships without requiring explicit governance structures."

The object of Ethereum Extitutional is the community of practice that has emerged within Ethereum through encounter and emergent alignment rather than formal engineering and rules, and long since been its lifeblood. 

Defying institutionalization, this cultural substrate generates abundant outputs from the free passions of its participants and the stigmergic coordination those passions produce. 

(In that respect, it's reminiscent of bottom up, rhizomatic cultures like hip hop or open source.)

At its healthiest, this space of extitutional production can exist in a kind of flow state where the autonomous passions of its participants are aligned and their thirst for experimentation is co-amplified.

However, here as in other extitutional spaces, there are a host of forces attempting at all times to compromise that state, to capture it’s productive chaos for one or another centralized script.  
  
It’s the task of Ethereum Extititutional to protect that extitutional flow state and amplify its power.  
  
Some ways we may go about this:  
  
- Identifying the properties of a healthy and uncaptured space of cultural (peer) production  
- Looking to Ethereum's past as well as the history of other p2p cultures (hip hop, psychedelic) to identify the types of individuals and organizations that aid and amplify this state  
- Cataloguing the identified strategies for extitutional amplification into “extitutional modes”  
- Developing a trust graph to put funding behind those extitutional modes, to the end of protecting and preserving Ethereum’s cultural substrate from capture.

Being an open inquiry into the role of extitutionality in Ethereum, the frameworks, language and strategies of this community may change under its feet; more than any specific semantic formation, this community is bound and guided by a feeling or intuition about what may be lost in the ascendance of a so-called "Ethereum Institutional."
