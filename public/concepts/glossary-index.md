# Glossary Index

Key terms drawn from the writings of Exeunt and the Open Protocol Research Group. Each entry contains a definition synthesized from the corpus and a "Related Research" section linking to texts where the term appears meaningfully.

---

## Organizational & Infrastructural Forms
- [[dao|DAO]] — Decentralized Autonomous Organization; the form that structurally enacts "the death of the ban"
- [[extitution|Extitution]] — Ephemeral institutional avatar that protects open protocols from capture
- [[substrate|Substrate]] — The permissionless social field from which organizational life emerges and draws legitimacy
- [[protocol-underground|Protocol Underground]] — Informal communities tethered by open protocols; extitutional dynamics dominant
- [[open-protocols|Open Protocols]] — Memetic, empirical knowledge sets that circulate freely through the social field

## Political & Economic Concepts
- [[undercapital|Undercapital]] — The virtual life of economic value; nondual potentiality as practical economic resource
- [[the-virtual|The Virtual]] — Real but not actual; the processual trace of potential within material dynamics
- [[ethereum-localism|Ethereum Localism]] — Reciprocal mimesis between web3 open-source culture and urban open cultures
- [[mutualism|Mutualism]] — Encounter-based, non-coercive cooperative practice; the extitutional form of mutual aid
- [[enclosure|Enclosure]] — Capture and privatization of commons, knowledge, and relational power
- [[control|Control]] — Organizational mode that mystifies network power as emanating from an interior source
- [[formalization-without-standardization|Formalization Without Standardization]] — Achieving coherence without imposing hegemonic standards
- [[overgrounding|Overgrounding]] — Making underground practices memeable without institutionalizing them
- [[d-acc|d/acc]] — Vitalik's philosophy of defensive/decentralized technological acceleration

## Philosophical & Ontological Frameworks
- [[flat-ontology|Flat Ontology]] — All categories of being are equally real; no metaphysical hierarchy
- [[field-ontology|Field Ontology]] — Shifting ontological attention adequate to the contingency of the encounter
- [[many-worlds|Many Worlds]] — Everett's MWI as philosophical resource for post-extractive design ethics
- [[the-virtual|The Virtual]] — Deleuzian concept; real but not actual; processual underside of the actual
- [[emanationism|Emanationism]] — Cosmological logic of top-down power; the deep structure of Control organizations
- [[imaginal-sovereignty|Imaginal Sovereignty]] — Reality-creation through exotic relations; transgression of administrative taboo
- [[undualing|Undualing]] — Reanimating diverse lifeworlds against the forces of ontological singularization

## Cognitive & Scientific Frameworks
- [[cognitive-security|Cognitive security]] — Protecting individual and collective sensemaking from adversarial manipulation; the epistemic dimension of sovereignty
- [[distributed-cognition|Distributed Cognition]] — Cognition distributed across organisms, environments, and socio-technical systems
- [[autopoiesis|Autopoiesis]] — Self-production as the operational definition of life and cognition (Maturana/Varela)
- [[open-ended-intelligence|Open-Ended Intelligence]] — Intelligence as enacted process of openness; Weinbaum/Veitas framework
- [[noopunk|Noöpunk]] — Aesthetic movement seeing the universe as a landscape of nested cognitive assemblages
- [[umwelt|Umwelt]] — An organism's species-specific subjective perceptual world (von Uexküll)
- [[transcendental-object-at-the-end-of-time|Transcendental Object at the End of Time]] — McKenna's naturalistic teleology; future attracting the present

## Cultural & Aesthetic Concepts
- [[aesthetics|Aesthetics]] — The political question of feeling and sensibility; ground of power and freedom
- [[assemblage|Assemblage]] — Heterogeneous combinations that produce emergent capacities (Deleuze/Guattari)
- [[haptic-vision|Haptic Vision]] — Embodied, reciprocal perception vs. the divisive Western optic gaze
- [[high-agency|High Agency]] — Mutual assumption of active shaping capacity; core underground value
- [[consent-culture|Consent Culture]] — Ongoing, explicit, situated agreement; the ground of extitutional life
- [[memeticism|Memeticism]] — Active encouragement of reproduction and divergent application; feature of open protocols
- [[egregore|Egregore]] — Collective psychic entity generated by group attention; candidate agent in posthuman economics

## Historical & Thinker References
- [[neotechnics|Neotechnics]] — Mumford's third technological epoch; electricity/alloys enabling decentralized production
- [[moloch|Moloch]] — Game-theoretic meme for coordination failures; critiqued for pre-rational ontological blindspot
- [[posthumanism|Posthumanism]] — Critique of humanist metaphysics in light of cybernetic and systems-theoretic discoveries
